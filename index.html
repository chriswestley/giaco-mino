About

Giaco Mino is a slow fashion studio in Copenhagen. Every piece begins as something that already exists: a worn shirt, a vintage hide, a length of deadstock cloth. Each one is remade by hand, one at a time. Nothing is made twice. When a piece is gone, it is gone.

The rhythm of 31

My grandfather, Giacomo, was born in Naples in 1931. Thirty-one years later, my mother was born. Thirty-one years after that, my sister. Three generations, each arriving on the same interval, each carrying something of the last into what came next.

The studio works to that rhythm. A garment arrives with a history. It is taken apart, kept where it matters, and made into something new. The past is not erased. It is carried forward.

The name

Giaco Mino is my grandfather's two names put together: Giaco, from Giacomo, and Mino, his nickname. On the back of every shirt in the first drop sit nine white buttons, for Giacomino.

How it is made

Only what already exists: vintage garments, old leather, deadstock fabric. Never new textiles. Every cut, seam and stitch is done by hand, here in the studio, by me. Materials are sourced as near to Denmark as possible, and always within the EU.

For those who care

These clothes are not made for everyone. They are for people who value craft over trend, who see beauty in imperfection, and who want to know where something came from, and who wore it before them.

— Mino
